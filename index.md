# Homepage of Jiachen Qian(钱佳琛) 
Welcome! I am a Postdoc in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of California San Diego, working with Prof. Yang Zheng. 
I received a Ph.D. degree in dynamical systems and control in the College of Engineering at Peking University, under the supervision of Prof. Zhisheng Duan, in 2024. I also received the B.S. degree in the same department in 2019. 
I am broadly interested in estimation, control, and learning of network systems, and their applications to large-scale systems such as power systems and traffic systems. My current research interests include:
- Online prediction for stochastic systems and optimality analysis
- Distributed perception of large-scale systems and the information flow over the network
- Data driven optimal controller design

Check out some exciting research in the SOC lab at UCSD.
